# Privacy Policy - Cubiqora: Block Puzzle

**Effective date:** October 23, 2026

Cubiqora is a small puzzle game made by one independent hobbyist developer. There is no
company, no team, and no server behind it. This page explains what data the app handles,
what it does **not** handle, and what Google's advertising service does when an ad is
shown.

## Summary

- The app does **not** collect personal data for the developer.
- All game progress and settings stay **on your device**.
- There are **no user accounts, no servers, and no analytics**.
- Ads are served by **Google AdMob**, which processes data under its own privacy policy.

## Data stored on your device

The following data is created by using the app and never leaves the device through any
code in the app:

| Data | Purpose | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Classic best score, Adventure progress, play statistics | Game progress | Local device storage (Android DataStore) |
| Sound, music, haptics, reduced-motion settings | Preferences | Local device storage |

You can delete all of it at any time with **Settings → Reset All Progress & Stats**.
Uninstalling the app removes it as well. Because nothing is uploaded, there is no copy
for the developer to delete.

## Permissions and what they are used for

| Permission | Why it is requested |
| --- | --- |
| `INTERNET` | Loading and displaying ads (Google AdMob). The game itself never uses the network. |
| `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Checking connectivity before requesting an ad, to avoid errors offline. |
| `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` | Reading the Google advertising ID so AdMob can serve and measure ads. You can reset or delete this ID in your Google settings. |
| `ACCESS_ADSERVICES_*` (Android 13+) | Privacy Sandbox ad topics and attribution APIs, used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK under your system consent. |
| `WAKE_LOCK`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` | Added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for ad playback. The app itself does not run background services. |

No camera, microphone, location, contacts, phone, or storage permissions are requested.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display:

- **Interstitial ads** — shown between completed runs (never during a run), and
- **Rewarded ads** — optionally watched for a one-time "second chance" continue.

When ads are shown, Google AdMob may collect and process:

- your advertising identifier and ad interaction data,
- IP address and coarse location derived from it (by Google),
- device and app information for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and measurement.

This processing is performed by Google under the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the
[Google AdMob policies](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/2753241).

I only see aggregate AdMob reports, such as total impressions and estimated earnings.
I cannot see who you are or which ads you personally saw.

Where consent is required (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland), the app
asks for it through Google's consent flow (UMP) before personalised ads are requested.
You can accept or decline, and you can change your choice later in **Settings → Privacy
Options**, which appears when Google requires it.

## Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. It
has no accounts and no community features. If you believe a child's data has been
processed through an ad, contact me at `Kenzory-studio@gmail.com` and I will help you
raise it with Google.
